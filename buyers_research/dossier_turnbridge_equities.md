# Turnbridge Equities - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: high overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Turnbridge Equities is a privately held New York developer-holder founded in 2015 by Andrew Joblon; it invests through discretionary vehicles (Turnbridge Equities Real Estate Fund I and Fund II LP) from 4 Bryant Park, Suite 200, New York, NY 10018, and no NJ office is found in public sources (pack: https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/turnbridge-equities; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1899002/000189900221000001/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml). It has been active in NJ since 2019-01, when it and investors advised by J.P. Morgan Asset Management paid $61M for the ~1M SF former Ballantine Brewery campus in Newark's Ironbound Opportunity Zone, recapitalized it with Long Wharf Capital at $92M in 2021-05, and sold the 738,000 SF "Newark Distribution Center" to Hines for $127.5M in 2023-01 (pack: https://therealdeal.com/new-york/2019/01/29/turnbridge-equities-buys-1m-sf-industrial-site-in-nj-opportunity-zone-for-61m/; https://njbiz.com/turnbridge-sells-newark-distribution-center-for-127-5m/). What it actually buys in NJ is port-adjacent and Turnpike-corridor warehouses and covered land: Newark Port submarket (615 Ferry St, 2022-06), Bayonne (169 Pulaski St, 2025-04) and Exit 8A (138 Georges Rd, South Brunswick, 2025-09) (https://www.turnbridgeeq.com/property_geography/new-jersey/). Since 2023 it also runs a national Industrial Outdoor Storage (IOS) strategy aimed at "New York City, Northern New Jersey, and Los Angeles" (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240306342512/en/Turnbridge-Equities-Acquires-2.5-acre-Industrial-Outdoor-Storage-IOS-Site-in-Brooklyn-New-York). Hold strategy is value-add: buy vacant, distressed or under-leased, spend $4M-$12M on docks, roof, solar and site work, lease up, then sell to core buyers or hold as covered land (pack; https://rebusinessonline.com/turnbridge-equities-begins-12m-repositioning-of-northern-new-jersey-industrial-complex/). A third-party profile puts roughly 65-70% of its portfolio in last-mile industrial and IOS (https://database.thesisdriven.com/developers/turnbridge-equities).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Priced NJ buys run $23.75M to $92M (2019-2025; 615 Ferry price not found), 143K SF to ~1.1M SF, roughly $55-$176/SF gross. They are single assets, usually multi-building campuses on 9-22 acres, not portfolios. The big Newark deals used institutional JV equity (J.P. Morgan AM 2019; Long Wharf 2021); the 2022-2025 deals are presented as Turnbridge alone, with Fund II the likely vehicle (INFERRED from Form D timing). Speed signals: 615 Ferry and 138 Georges were off-market, 138 Georges came out of foreclosure, 615 Ferry used seller financing, and Bayonne closed with an $80.27M JLL acquisition loan against the reported $91.5M price (https://www.roi-nj.com/2025/04/28/real_estate_development/jll-secures-80-27-million-acquisition-financing-for-bayonne-industrial-property/). They sold the Newark campus about 20 months after the 2021 recap.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Size: 140K-750K SF single building or campus, or a 2-4 acre IOS/truck-parking site; 9-22 acres of land preferred.
- Submarket: Newark/Ironbound/Port Newark, Bayonne/Jersey City, Elizabeth/Kearny, Exit 8A - within ~2 miles of a port terminal or at a Turnpike interchange.
- Tenancy: vacant, short-term leaseback, or under-market in-place rent they can reposition; fully leased only as covered land with redevelopment upside.
- Physical: older product is fine (26-28 ft clear, few docks) if the site allows added docks, outdoor storage, new roof and solar.
- Price: $20M-$95M all-in; lender-owned, foreclosure, estate or off-market situations; seller financing considered.
- Timing: closes with acquisition financing lined up (JLL) and starts capex at closing.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Is Fund II still deploying or is a successor fund raising, and how much NJ dry powder and what target hold period does the current vehicle have?
2. How much vacancy or short-term leaseback can you underwrite today, and which IOS, truck-parking or cold-storage profiles are in or out for NJ?
3. Which NJ submarkets are you targeting for 2026 beyond Newark, Bayonne and Exit 8A, and what is your minimum site size for an IOS deal here?
4. Who signs off on a $25M-$100M NJ acquisition, and what is a realistic timeline from first look to hard money?
5. Who sources NJ deals for you - Jason Davis, Ryan Nelson or a local acquisitions associate - and do you prefer off-market direct, broker-marketed or lender/foreclosure channels?
