# Realterm - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Realterm is an Annapolis, MD private fund manager (open- and closed-end logistics funds plus a credit arm) that buys "transportation-advantaged" logistics real estate "designed to move - not store - freight": truck terminals, cross-docks, transload, final-mile warehouses and industrial outdoor storage (IOS) (pack A row; https://www.realterm.com/investment-opportunities/). Capital is its own fund series (Realterm Logistics Income Fund; Fund V per pack notes) plus JV equity from J.P. Morgan Asset Management (https://www.realterm.com/news/realterm-and-j-p-morgan-acquire-1-75-msf-premier-logistics-portfolio/); it reports over $10B of portfolio assets (https://www.realterm.com/). No NJ office found in public sources. It has bought in NJ since at least 2020-11: small infill sites in the Meadowlands/Bergen (Carlstadt, East Rutherford, Fairview), the Port Newark-Linden corridor and I-287 (South Plainfield), 7,000-100,000 SF on 1.6-5.5 acres (pack deal rows). It is a value-add/core-plus holder that upgrades yards and lighting, then leases (https://www.realterm.com/news/realterm-acquires-northern-new-jersey-industrial-outdoor-storage-facility/).

## 2. Stated buy box
- Strategy: "value-add, core, opportunistic, core+ and credit logistics investments in top freight corridors" (https://www.realterm.com/investment-opportunities/).
- Product: "high flow through" logistics - truck terminals/cross-dock, transload, final-mile, IOS; buys "leased investments and vacant or short-term leased facilities" (same URL, via pack A row).
- Location: infrastructure-advantaged markets (seaport, airport, rail, highway); NJ releases stress Port Newark, Lincoln Tunnel/GWB and I-95/I-80 access (pack A row; https://www.realterm.com/news/realterm-acquires-880-890-paterson-plank-road-in-east-rutherford-n-j/).
- No published size, price, clear-height or age criteria - pattern inferred from deals: 7,000-100,000 SF on 1.6-5.5 acres, high door counts (28-52), paved lit yards, 20-ft clear acceptable; single assets and portfolios up to $277M.

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
NJ single-asset deals ran 6,960-100,627 SF on 1.6-5.5 acres; every NJ price was undisclosed. INFERRED from comparable Realterm buys - three Yellow Corp terminals for ~$16M total (https://news.ioslist.com/p/ioslist-feb-22-2024-realterm-buys-yellow-ios-realty-income-buys-sunbelt-portfolio), a $25M Pompano Beach IOS site (https://therealdeal.com/miami/2025/05/09/realterm-buys-pompano-beach-industrial-property-for-25m/), and ~$16M-$21M per property in portfolios ($111M/7 with J.P. Morgan; $277M/13) (https://www.commercialsearch.com/news/realterm-buys-ios-portfolio-for-111m/) - expect roughly $5M-$30M per NJ asset, far more for portfolios. Single assets come through local brokers (C&W, NAI Hanson); portfolios via Brookfield and the Yellow bankruptcy; equity is fund, J.P. Morgan JV, and a $350M credit fund for lending (https://www.connectmoney.com/stories/realterm-launches-350m-logistics-credit-fund-to-target-industrial-ios-lending/). Buys leased and vacant alike.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Truck terminal, cross-dock, transload, fleet-maintenance or IOS yard; plain warehouse only if final-mile with heavy door count.
- 1.5-10 acres, building 5,000-100,000 SF; low coverage, paved lit yard, deep truck courts; 20-ft clear acceptable.
- Bergen/Meadowlands, Port Newark-Elizabeth-Linden, Hudson waterfront, I-287 Middlesex; minutes to Lincoln Tunnel/GWB/I-95.
- Leased, short-term or vacant all work; value-add upgrades (lighting, ramps, paving) are a plus.
- Likely $5M-$30M single asset (inferred); bigger via portfolio or JV; will also lend if the owner wants debt, not a sale.
- Comfortable with carrier tenants and distressed sellers (Yellow bankruptcy).

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Which vehicle buys in NJ today (Income Fund, Fund V, J.P. Morgan JV), with how much dry powder and what hold period?
2. Will you take a vacant or month-to-month IOS yard or a cold facility in NJ, and what lease-up underwriting do you need?
3. Which NJ submarkets top the 2026 list - Meadowlands, Port Newark/Linden, I-287 - and are any now off?
4. Who signs off on an NJ deal and what is the realistic LOI-to-close timeline?
5. Who sources for you in NJ - brokers or off-market channels - and would you consider a loan rather than a purchase?
