# Venture One Real Estate - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Venture One is a Rosemont, IL industrial private-equity fund manager, developer and operator active in Chicago, the Northeast and Florida (https://gedc.com/business/venture-one-real-estate/). It buys NJ through closed-end, fully discretionary "VK Industrial" value-add funds co-sponsored with Chicago's Kovitz Investment Group; VK Industrial VI closed at $255M and VK Industrial VII (2024 vintage) is the active vehicle (pack: https://irei.com/news/venture-one-real-estate-holds-final-close-sixth-industrial-fund/; https://pitchbook.com/profiles/fund/26394-94F). No NJ office found; HQ is 9500 W Bryn Mawr Ave, Suite 340, Rosemont, IL 60018 (pack: https://www.dnb.com/business-directory/company-profiles/venture-one-real-estate-llc.2e47801165f531fc276aa73616823ca4). Since 2019-12 it has bought one or two NJ assets a year: 14K-107K SF small-bay and multi-tenant light industrial in the Meadowlands, Fairfield / West Caldwell, Totowa / Clifton and North Brunswick, plus a 7.1-acre Clifton industrial service site (rows below). The model is lease-up: 25 Commerce Rd, Fairfield was about 68% leased at purchase and 100% by 2025-02 (pack: https://rebusinessonline.com/ventureone-acquires-103008-sf-industrial-property-in-fairfield-new-jersey/).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Buildings run 14,300-107,107 SF (median about 50K SF) plus one 7.1-acre IOS site; the only disclosed NJ price is $17.8M (about $188/SF), so most buys are inferred at $3M-$25M. Every NJ deal is a single asset bought directly by the fund, all-cash per the pack, on discretionary capital. It has bought vacant (116 Lehigh), 68%-leased (25 Commerce) and 100%-leased buildings. No 1031 or sale-leaseback signals found.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- 15K-110K SF multi-tenant or small-bay light industrial; 7-acre-plus IOS / service sites also fit.
- Meadowlands (Carlstadt, Moonachie, Teterboro, Little Ferry), Fairfield / West Caldwell, Totowa / Clifton, North Brunswick.
- Any tenancy - leased, partly leased or vacant; lease-up is the model.
- 20'-24' clear, drive-ins plus a few docks; 1980s vintage acceptable.
- Roughly $3M-$25M single asset; all-cash discretionary fund capital.
- VK Industrial VII is deploying now (2025-2026 closings in NJ, NY, IL).
