# Dalfen Industrial - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: high overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Dalfen Industrial is a Dallas-based private-equity industrial investor (closed-end funds; operating company Dalfen Industrial LLC, 17304 Preston Road, Suite 550, Dallas, TX 75252) that calls itself one of the largest US industrial buyers and a last-mile leader (pack A row; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dalfen-industrial-continues-new-jersey-acquisitions-with-newly-developed-roxbury-property-302020532.html; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1883902/000188390222000001/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml). It buys through its own Dalfen Last Mile Industrial Fund series (Fund IV 2019, Fund V 2022) and with two partners: Goldman Sachs Asset Management on big-box and portfolios, and Centerbridge Partners-advised funds on a coastal industrial outdoor storage (IOS) program (pack entities; https://therealdeal.com/texas/2026/02/24/goldman-dalfen-venture-sells-new-jersey-warehouse-to-ice/; https://re-nj.com/dalfen-investors-buy-two-acre-industrial-outdoor-storage-parcel-in-edison/). No NJ office found; NJ sits in the Northeast region (NY, NJ, PA, DC) (https://www.dalfen.com/regions/northeast-region/). Active in NJ since 2023-05 with seven buys through 2024-12: new or vacant Class A warehouses of 92K-470K SF in Somerset, Morris, Bergen and Burlington counties, plus 1-7 acre IOS yards in Middlesex (Old Bridge, Sayreville, Edison) (pack deal rows). It operates what it owns but recycles capital: with Goldman it sold 38 warehouses to Starwood for ~$685M in 2025-03 and stayed on as operator (https://commercialobserver.com/2025/03/starwood-capital-buys-warehouses-goldman-sachs-dalfen/).

## 2. Stated buy box
- Product: "investment focus on strategically located urban infill warehouses and distribution buildings" (A row quote, 2023-12; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dalfen-industrial-continues-new-jersey-acquisitions-with-newly-developed-roxbury-property-302020532.html).
- Physical: efficient rectangular layouts configurable for single or multi-tenancy, auto/truck parking, 22 ft+ clear, generous loading ratios (A row notes, same source).
- Location: barriers to entry (no developable land, institutional ownership); near labor, rooftops and transportation corridors (A row notes).
- IOS (Centerbridge JV): major-roadway access, functional warehouse with multiple loading doors, favorable zoning, multi-user appeal; will "continue acquiring similar properties across the Northeast & Mid-Atlantic" (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dalfen-industrial--centerbridge-partners-continue-east-coast-industrial-outdoor-storage-acquisitions-302219194.html; https://www.roi-nj.com/2024/08/29/real_estate_development/dalfen-acquires-industrial-property-in-sayreville-for-4-05m-in-off-market-transaction/).
- Submarkets: Central and Northern NJ (A row). No published size or price band - INFERRED from deals: warehouses 90K-470K SF at $14M-$35M+; IOS yards 1-7 acres at $4M-$8M.

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Disclosed NJ prices run $4.05M and $7.6M (IOS) to $14.65M and $34.3M (warehouses, $157-$159/SF); Roxbury and Somerset were undisclosed. Warehouses were 92K-470K SF, IOS 1.3-6.9 acres; all seven were single-asset. Five of seven were off-market, bought from an owner-user (Raymour & Flanigan), merchant developers and a lender (Pangea) - they move on direct deals and accept vacancy on Class A. IOS goes into the Centerbridge JV; big-box can sit in a Goldman venture with Dalfen as minority owner (Roxbury). Portfolio checks with Goldman are $200M-$300M.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Warehouse: 80K-500K SF, built 2020+ or near-new Class A, 22 ft+ clear (Somerset was 36 ft), rectangular and divisible, ample car and trailer parking, in Bergen, Morris, Somerset, Middlesex or Burlington on I-287, I-80, the Turnpike or I-295.
- Vacancy is fine on Class A: Somerset, Lumberton and Roxbury were bought empty.
- IOS: 1-7 acre paved, fenced, lit yards with a small service building in Middlesex (Exit 10 / Route 35 / Route 9), zoned for truck and trailer storage - route to the Centerbridge JV.
- Price: $4M-$8M (IOS), $14M-$40M (single warehouse); $200M+ portfolios with Goldman.
- Sellers: owner-users, merchant developers, lenders - off-market, quick close.
- Timing: no NJ warehouse buy since 2024-12 and the Roxbury exit in 2026-02; confirm current NJ appetite first.
