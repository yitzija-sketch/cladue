# Seagis Property Group - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Privately held industrial developer-holder, founded 2005, HQ 100 Front Street, Conshohocken, PA, buying off its own balance sheet (pack A row, https://www.seagisproperty.com/). Owns 13M+ SF / ~200 buildings in NJ, NYC and South Florida; the NJ book is 6.3M+ SF across 40 logistics properties, concentrated in the Meadowlands, where the 2025-04 Carlstadt buy was its 15th investment (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/seagis-property-group-acquires-31-000-sf-industrial-building-in-northern-new-jersey-302441522.html). NJ office is in East Rutherford per a 2024 release (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/seagis-property-group-leases-202-000-square-foot-building-in-east-rutherford-nj-302179313.html); LinkedIn shows 160 Chubb Ave, Lyndhurst, unconfirmed (https://www.linkedin.com/company/seagis-property-group-lp). Pack deals since 2019-01 are 20,000-211,000 SF North and Central Jersey warehouses. Long-term holder: buys vacant or dated buildings, upgrades and leases them in-house, then refinances in pools ($122M JLL loan on 13 assets, 2023-01, pack entity row; $176M NJ/FL refinancing, 2026, https://re-nj.com/tag/seagis-property-group/).

- Submarket: Meadowlands first (Carlstadt, Moonachie, Secaucus, Lyndhurst, Kearny), then Ports / Exit 10-12 Middlesex and Newark.
- Tenancy: vacant, short-term, fully leased or owner-user exit all fit; user sale-leaseback plausible.

- Price: one comp at $151/SF (2022); INFERRED $3M-$32M; no published cap-rate target.
