# Brookfield Asset Management (Logistics) - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Closed-end private-equity real estate platform owned by Brookfield Corporation, run from Brookfield Place, 250 Vesey St, New York; no NJ office found in public sources (pack: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1504716/000110465923074357/tm2319608d1_40app.htm). It buys NJ through "Brookfield Asset Management-backed" opportunistic funds, operating arm Brookfield Properties and development subsidiary IDI Logistics (pack: https://www.roi-nj.com/2021/10/28/real_estate_development/cbre-facilitates-acquisition-of-19-acre-industrial-site-in-ridgefield-for-brookfield-asset-backed-fund/; https://idilogistics.com/press_release/idi-logistics-grows-new-jersey-portfolio/). Since 2020 it has bought infill development land, a rail-served CSX truck terminal, 42K-125K SF leased buildings in Bergen/Passaic and a 1.0M SF new park at Exit 7A (section 3). It is a buy-build-sell holder: it sold a 13-asset, 631,604 SF IOS portfolio including North Jersey to Realterm for $277M in 2025-01 (https://commercialobserver.com/2025/01/brookfield-sells-industrial-portfolio-realterm/), then took Peakstone Realty Trust private for ~$1.2B all-cash in 2026-05, adding 70+ assets, about 60 IOS, including a 6-acre yard at 1922 River Rd, Burlington NJ (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001600626/000114036126019333/ef20070245_ex99-1.htm; https://www.costar.com/article/188830973/brookfield-bets-big-on-industrial-with-acquisition-of-peakstone-realty-trust).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Disclosed NJ single-asset prices run $7.0M-$67.3M (average $29M) on 42K-125K SF buildings or 9-19 acre sites; Jackson was undisclosed but 2022's largest NJ trade. NJ buys are single assets closed by a fund, at least one off-market; portfolio moves are entity-level ($277M sale, $1.2B all-cash take-private). Pricing is land-driven ($1,150/SF at Kearny). No 1031 or sale-leaseback signals found.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Infill North/Central NJ: Bergen, Hudson, Passaic, I-287/Somerset, Exit 7A/8A.
- 9-20 acre development or rezoned-office land, rail/port-served yard or truck terminal; IOS via Peakstone.
- 40 ft clear Class A of 300K-1.0M SF, or 40K-125K SF leased infill with excess land.
- $7M-$70M single asset; larger only as portfolio or entity.
- Vacancy and entitlement risk acceptable; no 1031 clock.
- Off-market or brokered; expect a fund IC and all-cash close.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Which fund buys NJ today (BSREP V, a logistics vehicle, or Peakstone), how much is uncommitted, and what hold period?
2. After selling IOS to Realterm and buying Peakstone, is NJ IOS a buy, hold or sell in 2026, and what minimum yard size?
3. Will you take vacant or spec-leasing risk in NJ, and do you underwrite cold storage or rail-served product differently?
4. Which NJ submarkets are on the 2026 list beyond Bergen/Hudson and Exit 7A; is Central/South Jersey land in scope?
5. Who screens NJ deals - Andy Smith's team, Brookfield Properties or IDI - and what is the timeline from first look to IC approval?
