# Ambient Capital Partners - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Ambient Capital Partners is a privately owned, IOS-only investment and operating platform founded in 2022 that buys truck terminals, container yards and other low-coverage supply-chain sites, core through value-add (pack: https://irei.com/news/ambient-capital-partners-acquires-northern-new-jersey-industrial-outdoor-storage-portfolio-for-156m/; https://www.ambientcap.com/). No parent is disclosed; press calls it Chicago-based (pack entity row: Winnetka / Chicago, IL), its contact page lists San Francisco, and at least one deal used JV equity from an unnamed "institutional equity partner" (https://www.ambientcap.com/contact; https://www.bylinebank.com/insights/byline-banks-commercial-real-estate-group-funds-3-25m-loan-for-acquisition-of-inland-empire-industrial-outdoor-storage-property/). No NJ office found in public sources. Its only NJ trade is a 2024-10 six-site, 48.7-acre, $156.3M port-adjacent IOS portfolio in Harrison and Kearny (Hudson County), bought via NAI James E. Hanson and called possibly NJ's largest IOS deal (pack: https://www.naihanson.com/news/nai-james-e-hanson-negotiates-156-3m-northern-new-jersey-industrial-outdoor-storage-portfolio-sale/). It then hired Blau & Berg to lease about 650,000 SF at the Supor Blvd and Manor Ave sites, so it holds and operates (pack: https://re-nj.com/ambient-capital-taps-blau-berg-to-lease-650000-sq-ft-industrial-complex-in-harrison/). Hold: core-plus (pack, INFERRED).

## 2. Stated buy box
- Product: "targets core, core-plus and value-add industrial outdoor storage properties such as truck terminals, container yards and related facilities" (2024-10, https://irei.com/news/ambient-capital-partners-acquires-northern-new-jersey-industrial-outdoor-storage-portfolio-for-156m/).
- Geography: IOS "in strategic supply chain markets" in the US and Canada (https://www.ambientcap.com/); Hudson County NJ, Inland Empire and Texas evidenced by deals.
- Approach: "local execution, active asset management with a tenant focus, and a disciplined IOS-only investment strategy" (https://www.ambientcap.com/).
- Size, price, clear height, age, tenancy: no published criteria - pattern inferred from deals: 2 to 49 acres, leased yards with some building, single sites or portfolios, roughly $3M to $156M; stabilized tenancy INFERRED (pack flags it as such).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
One NJ price point: $156.3M for 48.7 acres, about $3.21M per acre (pack market_mentions, computed from NAI Hanson figures). Elsewhere it goes far smaller: a 2-acre Ontario, CA yard with a $3.25M Byline Bank loan in JV with an institutional partner (Byline URL above), and the 23.2-acre Texas terminal with LaSalle (Partners URL above). ## 7. What would make a property match them
- Product: truck terminal, container/chassis yard, trailer parking or low-coverage site; a leasable building is fine (they are marketing 650,000 SF).
- Location: port-adjacent Hudson/Essex/Union - Harrison, Kearny, Newark, Elizabeth - within about 10 miles of Port Newark-Elizabeth, Turnpike/Route 280 access.
- Size: 2 to 50 acres; portfolios a plus.
- Tenancy: trucking/logistics users, ideally long-term NNN; some vacancy tolerated.
- Price: roughly $3M to $160M; benchmark about $3.2M per acre (Harrison/Kearny, 2024).
- Timing: JV equity implies committee approval; marketed-deal timeline, not a 30-day close.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Is Harrison/Kearny held in a JV (LaSalle or another partner), how much discretionary equity is left for NJ in 2026-2027, and what is the target hold?
2. How has the 650,000 SF Blau & Berg lease-up gone, and does it change your appetite for NJ sites with vacant buildings versus pure paved yards?
3. Beyond Hudson County, which NJ submarkets (Elizabeth/Linden, Newark, Meadowlands, Exit 8A, South Jersey) are on the 2026 list, and what is your minimum NJ site size and price?
4. Who approves a deal - Azar and Hackett alone or the equity partner too - and what is a realistic LOI-to-close timeline on a $10M-$50M NJ yard?
5. Who sources for you in NJ today (NAI Hanson or others), and do you want off-market owner-user yards, trucking sale-leasebacks or covered-land plays?
