# Ares Management Real Estate (Ares Industrial REIT / legacy Black Creek) - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: high overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Ares Industrial Real Estate Income Trust (AIREIT) is a non-traded REIT, formerly Black Creek Industrial REIT IV, owned and advised by Ares Management Corp. (NYSE: ARES), HQ Los Angeles, REIT offices in Denver; no NJ office found in public sources (pack: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1625941/000162828026015438/aire-20251231.htm). Since 2025-12 the Ares logistics business operates as Marq Logistics (https://www.bisnow.com/national/news/industrial/ares-management-launches-marq-to-unify-its-logistics-real-estate-platform-132098). In NJ it has bought since at least 2019: Exit 7A warehouses and Burlington County land as Black Creek, Class A bulk off I-80 in Wayne (2022) and a 14-building South Jersey light-industrial portfolio (2024). As of 2026-06 it owns 17 NJ buildings, about 3.5M SF, 98% leased (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001625941/000162828026056338/aire-20260630.htm). Core-plus, long-hold: the 10-K talks of "building a national industrial warehouse operating company" (pack, 10-K above).

## 2. Stated buy box
- Product/tenancy: "high-quality distribution warehouses and other industrial properties that are leased to creditworthy corporate customers"; creditworthy does not mean investment grade and most customers are unrated (pack: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1625941/000162828026015438/aire-20251231.htm).
- Submarkets (pack A row): I-80 corridor (Wayne/Passaic), Southern NJ (Cinnaminson/Pennsauken), Northern NJ.
- Structures (pack A row): all-cash, portfolios, JV - the Ares/PSP venture of up to $2.4B (2026-09) is seeded with NJ assets and sourced by Marq Logistics (https://commercialobserver.com/2026/09/ares-management-psp-investments-logistics-partnership/).
- No published size, price, clear-height or age criteria - pattern INFERRED from deals: stabilized Class A bulk of 170K-560K SF, $100M+ multi-building portfolios, approved land only.

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Building deals ran $22.6M to $138.5M: single assets of 172K-557K SF and one 1.12M SF portfolio, at $106/SF (older multi-tenant South Jersey) to $249/SF (new I-80 bulk), against a $202/SF northern NJ average in 2021 (pack: https://re-nj.com/investor-demand-for-industrial-space-soaring-to-new-heights-as-rents-rise-at-record-pace/). All-cash balance-sheet buyer backed by a Wells Fargo credit facility (pack: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1625941/000155837025007173/aire-20250331xex10d1.htm), now adding JV capital. Low-profile: the Wayne price surfaced via SEC filing and the buyer would not name the address. No 1031 or sale-leaseback signals found.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Single asset 150K-600K SF Class A or near-new bulk, or a 10+ building light-industrial portfolio of 500K SF+.
- I-80/Route 46 (Wayne/Passaic), Exit 7A/8A, South Jersey (Camden/Burlington) along Routes 73/130.
- Stabilized, 90%+ leased to corporate tenants on multi-year terms; credit need not be rated.
- Modern bulk specs (Wayne traded at $249/SF); functional 1980s-2000s product acceptable in a portfolio at roughly $100-130/SF.
- Check of $20M-$150M, all-cash, no financing contingency; larger portfolios can run through the PSP JV.
- Approved land only; no entitlement risk seen in the record.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. How much undeployed capital does the PSP JV hold for the Northeast, and is AIREIT still buying NJ assets on its own balance sheet in 2026?
2. What vacancy, IOS or cold-storage exposure will Marq underwrite today, and at what discount to a stabilized price?
3. Which NJ submarkets are on your 2026-2027 target list beyond Wayne, Exit 7A and Camden/Burlington?
4. Who signs off - the Marq investment committee, AIREIT's board or PSP - and what is the usual LOI-to-close timeline?
5. Who sources NJ deals for you (in-house Northeast team, CBRE, Lee & Associates), and do you still prefer unmarketed trades like Wayne?
