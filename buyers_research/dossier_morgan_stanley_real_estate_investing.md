# Morgan Stanley Real Estate Investing - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Morgan Stanley Real Estate Investing (MSREI) is the private real estate arm of Morgan Stanley Investment Management. It buys through closed-end value-add funds (North Haven Real Estate Fund series), an open-end core fund (Prime Property Fund LLC) and a non-traded net-lease REIT (North Haven Net REIT), all managed from 1585 Broadway, New York, NY 10036 (pack entity rows; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1999784/000199978425000017/nh-20241231.htm). No NJ office was found; NJ work runs through operating partner Saxum Real Estate of Summit, NJ. The pack shows one NJ purchase: the 7-building, 1.2M SF Murray Road / Ridgedale Avenue light-industrial campus in East Hanover (Morris County), bought 2023-11 from Urban Edge Properties for $217.5M, 100% leased to 13 tenants (https://www.jll.com/en-us/newsroom/jll-arranges-largest-industrial-sale-of-the-year-in-nj). Since then it bought a Nissan-leased building in Somerset via the REIT (2025) and sold a 450,330 SF Exit 8A warehouse (2026-03). Firm-wide, MSREI says it bought about $1.5B of U.S. industrial in 2025 and holds over 75M SF (https://www.morganstanley.com/press-releases/morgan-stanley-real-estate-investing-acquires-advanced-manufacturing-facility).

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Size: 300,000 SF+ single asset or 500,000 SF-1.2M SF multi-building campus; sub-$50M one-offs are off pattern.
- Price: roughly $100M-$350M; $150-$500/SF depending on tenancy.
- Submarkets: Morris / Essex (Route 10, I-280), Somerset (I-287), Exit 8A / Turnpike corridor.
- Tenancy: stabilized - fully leased multi-tenant light industrial, or single-tenant 10-year+ net lease (sale-leasebacks fit the REIT).
- Physical: 21 ft clear was acceptable for multi-tenant, 36 ft for bulk; heavy parking a plus.
- Timing: marketed processes are fine; expect institutional diligence with Saxum doing local work.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Which vehicle buys NJ industrial today - North Haven Fund X or a successor, Prime Property Fund, or North Haven Net REIT - and what dry powder and hold period does each have?
2. After selling Exit 8A in 2026-03, are you a net buyer or seller of NJ bulk, and which 2026 submarkets (Morris, Somerset, Meadowlands, Exit 8A) are you targeting?
3. Will you underwrite vacancy, IOS or cold storage, or only stabilized multi-tenant and long-term net lease?
4. Is Saxum your exclusive NJ operator - must a deal come through them, or can a broker bring it to the New York team directly?
5. Who signs off (IC cadence, approval thresholds) and what is a realistic LOI-to-close timeline on a $100M+ NJ asset?
