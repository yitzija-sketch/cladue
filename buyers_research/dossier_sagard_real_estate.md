# Sagard Real Estate - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: high overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Denver-based US real estate adviser owned by Sagard (Power Corporation of Canada); formerly EverWest Real Estate Investors (rebranded 2023-10), so earlier NJ deals closed as EverWest (https://www.sagard.com/blog/2023/10/16/everwest-real-estate-investors-rebrands-as-sagard-real-estate/). ~$6.0B AUM in an NFI-ODCE core open-end fund, separate accounts, a newer "moderate value-add" open-end fund, and a US$360M-target industrial outdoor storage (IOS) JV with La Caisse launched 2026-03 (https://www.sagard.com/blog/2025/01/08/sagard-real-estate-expands-industrial-portfolio-with-acquisition-of-19-commerce-court-west-in-south-brunswick-nj/; https://www.lacaisse.com/en/news/pressreleases/sagard-real-estate-caisse-launch-industrial-outdoor-storage-joint-venture). Nearest office is New York City; no NJ office found (https://www.sagard.com/real-estate/team/). In NJ since at least 2019, buying 47K-224K SF single-tenant warehouses in the Meadowlands, Avenel/Woodbridge, Exit 8A and Rahway, plus a Woodmont development JV; long-hold capital.

- No published size / price bands - inferred from deals: 45K-225K SF, $19M-$66M, $250-$410/SF, stabilized or new.

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Disclosed 2019-2024 prices run $19.4M-$65.9M (median ~$37M) on 47K-215K SF at $288-$410/SF; Monmouth Junction 2026 ($56.9M, $254/SF) tops the band and the IOS yards (~$10M) sit below it. Almost all single-asset; one three-building portfolio (2021). Structures: all-cash (Rahway 2022), two manufacturer sale-leasebacks (2019, 2022), JV equity for IOS (La Caisse) and development (Woodmont). Speed signals: buys from merchant builders right after lease-up (Deugen, Woodmont), works marketed JLL / Newmark / CBRE / C&W processes, accepts near-term rollover (Avenel).

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Size: 45K-225K SF single building; 50K-120K SF is the repeat zone.
- Location: Exit 8A, Woodbridge-Avenel, Rahway, Meadowlands (East Rutherford, Moonachie, Secaucus).
- Tenancy: one distribution or manufacturing user, 100% leased; sale-leasebacks welcome; 12-18 month rollover fits the value-add fund.
- Physical: new or renovated, 26'-32'+ clear, dock-high loading, trailer parking, ESFR or a path to it; Class B with an upgrade plan.
- IOS: fenced, paved 1-5 ac yards in Bergen / Hudson / Union near the port, around $7M/ac.
- Price: $15M-$70M, ~$250-$410/SF; all-cash capability.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Which vehicle buys in NJ today - ODCE core, the value-add open-end fund, or the La Caisse IOS JV - and how much is left to deploy in each this year?
2. What hold period does the value-add fund underwrite, and at what in-place occupancy will you still bid?
3. Are Exit 8A and the Meadowlands the 2026-2027 focus, or would you look at Exit 7A, Route 78 or South Jersey?
4. How much vacancy, cold storage or heavy manufacturing will you take, and what is the smallest IOS yard you will buy?
5. Who screens NJ deals first (Terentjev, Good, or the NYC office), how fast can you reach IC, and do you prefer marketed processes or off-market developer calls?
