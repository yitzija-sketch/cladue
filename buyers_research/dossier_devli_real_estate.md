# Devli Real Estate - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Devli Real Estate (also "Devli Group") is a privately held, family-run investment, development and management company founded in 1986 that owns industrial, retail, residential, office, self-storage and hospitality assets across 12 states (https://devli.com/company). It buys with its own balance sheet, not a fund (INFERRED). Its office is 800 Ave at Port Imperial, Suite 3, Weehawken, NJ; trade press calls it "Wallington-based" because its 34-acre assemblage is there (https://devli.com/contact; https://re-nj.com/devli-acquires-wallington-industrial-site-assembling-34-acre-development-parcel/). It has bought NJ industrial since at least 2018 (https://www.connectcre.com/stories/devli-group-buys-hackettstown-commerce-park-in-warren-county-nj/) and since 2021 has focused on the Meadowlands (East Rutherford, Carlstadt, Moonachie, Wallington), Bergen/Hudson infill and a Newark IOS site. It is a long-term holder that assembles adjoining parcels for future development and buys small/mid-bay buildings to lease up.

## 2. Stated buy box
- Product (stated): "Devli acquires Industrial, Office, Retail, Multifamily, Self Storage, Land and other Specialized Assets" across "submarkets and property types where they believe they can create long term value" (https://devli.com/acquisitions).
- Strategy (stated 2026): "pursuing infill industrial and flex assets in supply-constrained Northeast submarkets" - Christopher Devli (https://nyrej.com/devli-real-estate-acquires-67000-sf-flex-industrial-property-on-rte-17-in-upper-saddle-river).
- Size, price, clear height, age, tenancy: no published criteria - pattern inferred from deals: buildings 7,200-175,000 SF; land 4-34 acres, preferably next to existing holdings; vacant or short-term sale-leaseback accepted; 24-26 ft clear is fine; IOS / crane-served considered.

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Only two prices are public - $1.05M for a 7,200 SF condo and about $60M for 26.6 acres - so the band is roughly $1M-$60M, with buildings of 7,200-175,000 SF and sites of 4-34 acres. Devli does single assets and small bundles (a 4-property add in one month; a 3-parcel assemblage over time). Structures seen: sale-leaseback, JV with a local developer (Deugen), off-market land, and bank debt (Provident Bank, 2026). It is a patient repeat buyer of neighbours to its own holdings.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Location: Meadowlands (Carlstadt, East Rutherford, Moonachie, Wallington), Bergen/Hudson infill, GWB and Route 17 corridors, Newark port-adjacent.
- Size: 7,000-175,000 SF buildings, or 4-30 acre sites - especially parcels touching 520 Main Ave Wallington or the Carlstadt assemblage.
- Tenancy: vacant, short-term SLB or multi-tenant shallow-bay all acceptable.
- Physical: older low-coverage infill, drive-in heavy, crane-served or IOS-capable; 24-26 ft clear is enough.
- Price: $1M-$60M observed; single buildings likely $5M-$30M (INFERRED).
- Timing: off-market and assemblage deals; bank-financed closings.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Is the 34-acre Wallington assemblage heading to ground-up development in 2026-2027, and does that shift your appetite from land to leased buildings?
2. What is your equity source and target hold - family balance sheet held indefinitely, or a programmatic partner with a hold period?
3. After Newark and Upper Saddle River, how much vacancy, IOS or cold-storage exposure will you take on a single asset?
4. Which submarkets beyond the Meadowlands are 2026 targets - GWB/Route 17, Exit 8A, Newark/Elizabeth, or out of state?
5. Who sources and signs off - Mark Devli as CIO alone, or a committee - and what is your usual LOI-to-close timeline?
