# Faropoint - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: high overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Faropoint is a Hoboken-based closed-end private-equity fund manager that buys small-bay / last-mile warehouses; founded 2012, it reports 35M+ SF and 600+ warehouses acquired and says ~2% of sourced deals close (pack A row; https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/12/05/2992705/0/en/Faropoint-Announces-195-421-SF-Class-A-Industrial-Development-Project-in-Strategic-New-Jersey-Location.html). Capital is institutional: Fund III closed at $915M in 2024-06 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240626350875/en/Faropoint-Closes-Flagship-Urban-Industrial-Value-Fund-III-with-%24915-Million-Exceeding-Target-Fund-Size-and-Attracting-Top-Tier-Global-Investors), Fund IV launched 2025-06 targeting $1B with $225M seed from Teacher Retirement System of Texas (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250625536421/en/Faropoint-Launches-Flagship-Industrial-Value-Fund-IV-Targeting-$1-Billion), and Invesco Real Estate took a minority stake in the platform in 2023-11 (https://roi-nj.com/2023/11/29/real_estate/faropoint-secures-strategic-investment-from-invesco-real-estate). HQ and NJ office are one: 111 River Street, Suite 1010, Hoboken NJ 07030 (Form D; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2069751/000199419325000004/0001994193-25-000004-index.htm). It entered North Jersey in 2021-12 with the $132.5M Kushner portfolio and has since bought multi-tenant small-bay parks and single last-mile buildings in Bergen (Mahwah, Allendale, Oakland, Moonachie, Fair Lawn), Morris (Whippany, Rockaway, Wharton, Florham Park) and Somerset (Branchburg), plus owner-occupier sale-leasebacks in Burlington and Mercer. Hold strategy is value-add: buy below-market in-place rents, mark to market, light capex (LED, roofs), fund-level revolver financing (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211222005173/en/Faropoint-Acquires-10-Building-Last-Mile-Portfolio-In-Northern-New-Jersey-From-Kushner-Cos.-For-$132.5M).

## 2. Stated buy box
- **Size:** "small-bay warehouses ranging from 20,000 to 100,000 square feet", suites 20,000-40,000 SF (A row quote, 2024-12; https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/12/05/2992705/0/en/Faropoint-Announces-195-421-SF-Class-A-Industrial-Development-Project-in-Strategic-New-Jersey-Location.html); Fund IV repeats it and targets 200-250 assets (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250625536421/en/Faropoint-Launches-Flagship-Industrial-Value-Fund-IV-Targeting-$1-Billion).
- **Price:** deals "averaging under $10M that institutional capital overlooks" (A row notes, same source); the A row's $10M max is unverifiable and NJ portfolio buys run far larger (section 3).
- **Product / strategy:** small-bay, shallow-bay, last-mile infill; Fund IV's "primary strategy remains mark-to-market, particularly in markets where in-place rents are materially below market", plus lease-up and a development sleeve (BusinessWire 2025-06-25, above). ROI-NJ called it "the real estate investment firm that loves Class B industrial" (https://www.roi-nj.com/2022/02/07/real_estate/faropoint-the-reit-that-loves-class-b-industrial-gobbling-up-south-jersey-properties/).
- **Submarkets:** Northern NJ - Meadowlands, Morris, Bergen - plus Atlanta, Dallas, Philadelphia, Chicago, Tampa, Miami, Baltimore, Memphis (A row).
- **Tenancy / structures:** multi- or single-tenant ("either"); portfolios, all-cash, sale-leasebacks. A separate Industrial Sale-Leaseback Fund targets infill industrial 20,000-200,000 SF, ~10-year average lease term, $150M credit line (https://www.roi-nj.com/2024/10/21/real_estate/faropoint-secures-150m-line-with-for-industrial-sale-leaseback-fund/).
- **Clear height / age:** not found in public sources.

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Priced NJ single-asset buys (computed from the rows): $1.6M-$23.75M, 17,600-151,000 SF, $91-$285/SF, median about $9.6M - matching its "under $10M average" line; Florham Park 2025 was $9.25M. Portfolios are the exception: $28.3M, $132.5M and $144.5M in NJ, $328M multi-state. Nearly everything was 98-100% leased at close; two were owner-occupier sale-leasebacks (Delran, Delanco). It buys through listing brokers (C&W, CBRE, JLL, Meridian, NAI Mertz, WCRE) and has used C&W as buyer's rep. Financing is fund-level - a KeyBank revolver on Kushner, a $600M Fund IV acquisition facility in 2026-01 (https://www.roi-nj.com/2026/01/08/finance/faropoint-announces-600m-acquisition-credit-facility-secured-for-industrial-value-fund-iv/) - so it behaves like a cash buyer. Fund IV had deployed ~$284M across 30 properties / 1.72M SF by 2026-02 (https://news.smallbaylist.com/p/february-24-apg-makes-largest-commitment-faropoin-industrial-value-fund). Closing speed not quantified in public sources.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- **Size:** 20,000-100,000 SF, or multi-tenant parks divisible to 20,000-40,000 SF suites; up to ~150,000 SF single-tenant (Branchburg) and 200,000 SF for a sale-leaseback.
- **Submarket:** Bergen (Mahwah/Oakland/Meadowlands), Morris (Rockaway/Whippany/Florham Park), Somerset/Route 22, near I-80/I-95/I-287; South Jersey only as a sale-leaseback or small last-mile.
- **Tenancy:** leased, in-place rents materially below market, near-term rollover; 98-100% occupancy is the norm, vacancy appetite unproven in NJ.
- **Physical:** 1970s-90s Class B shallow-bay / flex-warehouse, functional not trophy; clear-height specs not found in public sources.
- **Price:** $2M-$25M single assets ($90-$290/SF historically); portfolios ~$30M-$150M+ get fund attention.
- **Timing / structure:** owner-occupier sale-leasebacks (~10-year term) fit the SLB fund; family-trust and legacy owners (Ilaria, Oakland Management, Alexander) are repeat seller types; fund revolver means no financing contingency.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. How much of Fund IV's $1B target is closed and undeployed today, and what share is earmarked for Northern NJ versus the other eight markets through 2027?
2. Would you take meaningful vacancy or a short-WALT asset in Bergen/Morris for the lease-up sleeve, and does the development sleeve mean you will now buy land or covered-land in NJ?
3. Does the Sale-Leaseback Fund still have capacity for NJ owner-occupiers, and what minimum lease term, credit and size do you require?
4. Who signs off - Itay Ron for the Northeast, then an investment committee? - and what is your typical timeline from first look to hard money?
5. Who sources for you in NJ today: only C&W/CBRE/JLL listings, or do you pay for direct off-market introductions from tenant-rep and local brokers?
