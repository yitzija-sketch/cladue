# The STRO Companies - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
The STRO Companies is a private, family-controlled developer-holder founded in 1994 by Steven Millstein for the Millstein family office; it says it owns about 4M SF of industrial, flex and multifamily in Northern NJ and Florida (pack: https://stro.com/about/; https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-stro-companies). HQ is 104 Chestnut Street, Suite 300, Ridgewood, NJ 07450, a building STRO owns; it also has Manhattan and Tampa offices (pack: LinkedIn page). Capital is the family balance sheet plus bank debt, with Kushner Real Estate Group (KRE) as a recurring JV partner on larger value-add and redevelopment plays (pack: https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/stro-co-break-ground-to-turn-new-jersey-data-center-into-warehouse/). In NJ it has bought steadily since at least 2019, almost entirely 45,000-141,000 SF Class B warehouses and small-bay buildings in an Essex-Passaic-Bergen-Morris cluster: Fairfield (three buildings), Clifton, Passaic, Mahwah, Florham Park, Woodland Park and, in 2026, Elmwood Park (deal rows; searches). It is a long-term holder that refinances rather than sells - it has owned its 353,000 SF Wayne building 30+ years (https://re-nj.com/stro-truist-close-25-million-loan-to-refi-353000-sq-ft-wayne-industrial-building/).

## 2. Stated buy box
- Stated: STRO "pursues new Value-Add and Stabilized small to medium size industrial real estate acquisitions in Northern New Jersey" (https://stro.com/about/).
- Stated 2026-09: focused on "buying well-located industrial properties in supply-constrained submarkets" (pack note; https://www.roi-nj.com/2026/09/10/real_estate_development/stro-kre-acquire-elmwood-park-industrial-property-complete-13-acre-assemblage/).
- Stated 2024 (Fitch): wants more fully leased small-bay like 97 Fairfield Rd, bought in a "quick, expedited closing with a long-standing seller" (https://www.roi-nj.com/2024/08/13/real_estate_development/stro-cos-acquires-76560-sq-ft-industrial-facility-in-fairfield/); also more northern NJ and Southeast warehouses, with "a considerable amount of capital to deploy" (re-nj Truist article above).
- No published size, price, clear-height or age criteria. Inferred from deals: 45,000-141,000 SF; Class B; 22 ft clear acceptable; small-bay multi-tenant (8,000-13,000 SF units) or single-tenant; vacant or obsolete buildings acceptable when KRE partners (data center to warehouse; vacant complex to IOS plus sale).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Only three prices surfaced ($7.3M, ~$8.35M, a disputed $13.6M) plus a $10.8M purchase loan, so the inferred check is roughly $5M-$15M per asset for the core 45,000-141,000 SF buys (about $50-$180/SF). Bigger plays - Woodland Park (16 ac, $60M refi) and the Elmwood Park assemblage - are done only in JV with KRE. Deals are single assets bought one at a time but clustered near existing holdings (three in Fairfield; Clifton/Passaic; three parcels on one Elmwood Park street); the only portfolio was two Clifton buildings. All are bank-financed (IDB, Valley, Truist, Capital One, City National, Kearny, Signature; pack and press); no 1031 or sale-leaseback noted. Speed signals: an "expedited closing" with a long-term owner (2024) and off-market buys in Florida (https://www.roi-nj.com/2024/09/05/real_estate_development/stro-kre-group-acquire-infill-logistics-property-in-tampa-airport-submarket/).

## 7. What would make a property match them
- 40,000-150,000 SF Class B warehouse or small-bay/multi-tenant; 20 ft+ clear is fine (22 ft at 97 Fairfield Rd).
- Northern NJ infill: Fairfield/Route 46, Clifton-Passaic-Wayne, Elmwood Park/I-80 Bergen, Mahwah/Route 17, Florham Park; near an existing STRO building is a plus.
- Tenancy either way: fully leased small-bay for cash flow, or vacant/obsolete with a redevelopment or outdoor-storage angle (bigger ones as KRE JV deals).
- Price roughly $5M-$20M single-asset; larger only as an assemblage or redevelopment with a partner.
- Sellers wanting certainty and speed: family balance sheet, relationship banks, repeat "expedited" closings.
- Covered-land / IOS sites with yard (the 30 Paul Kohner Pl conversion).

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. How much family-office capital is earmarked for NJ purchases over the next 12 months, and at what deal size do you bring in KRE rather than buy alone?
2. After converting 30 Paul Kohner Place to outdoor storage, is IOS or low-coverage land now a standalone target, and would you take cold storage or heavy-manufacturing buildings?
3. Beyond Fairfield, Clifton/Passaic and Elmwood Park, which submarkets are priorities for 2026-2027 - Newark port district, Mahwah/Route 17, Morris County, elsewhere?
4. What is the approval path and typical LOI-to-close timeline (Fitch / Eli Millstein to Steven Millstein?), and how much diligence time do you need on a fully leased small-bay deal?
5. Who sources for you today - brokers you repeat with (JLL, C&W, McBride, NAI Hanson), direct owner outreach - and how do you handle fees on off-market introductions?
