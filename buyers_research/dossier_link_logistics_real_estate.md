# Link Logistics Real Estate - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Blackstone's US logistics operating platform, formed 2019 and funded by Blackstone's open-end vehicles (BREIT, BREP) (pack: https://re-nj.com/link-logistics-unveils-new-164000-sq-ft-warehouse-at-kearny-redevelopment-site/). HQ New York; operating HQ 602 W. Office Center Dr, Fort Washington, PA; Edison, NJ field office per aggregators (low confidence, pack). Its site lists 61 NJ warehouses (https://www.linklogistics.com/properties-for-lease/east-region/new-jersey/), much inherited from predecessor Colony Industrial (pack: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20190326005307/en/Colony-Industrial-Acquires-Light-Industrial-Warehouses-Northern). Deal rows show two plays: second-generation port/infill warehouses (Linden, Mahwah) and ~10-acre obsolete sites redeveloped into 120,000-165,000 SF buildings (Kearny, Parsippany). Core-plus, long hold, but a net seller nationally in 2025-2026, "refining its national portfolio" (https://www.commercialsearch.com/news/link-logistics-sells-new-jersey-asset/).

## 2. Stated buy box
- Quote: "focuses on in-fill locations located in large and mature urban areas"; avoids big-box far outside city centers (https://www.freightwaves.com/news/with-blackstones-bucks-behind-it-link-logistics-makes-its-presence-known).
- No published size, price, clear-height or age criteria. **INFERRED from deals:** Kearny/Meadowlands, Linden/port, Bergen, I-80/I-287 Parsippany; 100,000-275,000 SF warehouses; ~10-acre redevelopment sites; all-cash; single asset or small portfolio (pack: https://re-nj.com/jll-link-logistics-eyes-123000-sq-ft-warehouse-after-acquiring-parsippany-office-parcel/).
- New 2026 vertical: industrial outdoor storage via a JV with Transport Properties, led by Jack Hennessey (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260202152331/en/Link-Logistics-Announces-Leadership-Changes-to-Drive-Continued-Growth); no NJ IOS buy found in public sources.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Hudson/Bergen/Union/Essex/Middlesex infill or I-80/I-287 Morris; not exurban big-box.
- 100,000-300,000 SF warehouse, or 8-15 acre covered-land / obsolete office site for 120,000-170,000 SF.
- Leased second-generation stock works; vacancy or redevelopment accepted in prime locations.
- $20M-$60M single asset; portfolios above $75M.
- Truck/trailer yards for the IOS JV (NJ appetite unverified).
- Net-seller posture now, so lead with the infill story; expect a Fort Washington, PA signature block.
