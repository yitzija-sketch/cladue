# Longpoint Realty Partners - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Longpoint (manager Longpoint Partners LP, trade name Longpoint Realty Partners) is a Boston-based, SEC-registered private-equity real estate firm founded 2015 that buys through closed-end value-add funds; ~$3.9B regulatory AUM in 2024 (pack: https://adviserinfo.sec.gov/firm/summary/283800). Fund II closed at $669M in 2021-11 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211118006078/en/Longpoint-Realty-Partners-Closes-Second-Fund-With-U.S.-Global-Investors), Fund III at ~$940M (https://www.costar.com/article/2072279920/longpoint-closes-third-industrial-fund-with-940-million), and Fund IV opened 2025-12 with a first close expected 2026-04, targeting industrial, multifamily and manufactured housing (https://realassets.ipe.com/news/illinois-municipal-invests-in-crow-and-longpoint-us-real-estate-funds/10135567.article). HQ is 116 Huntington Ave, Boston; the NJ office is in East Rutherford, listed as One Meadowlands Plaza, Suite 200, ~7 staff, reported moving 2025-08 - verify (https://www.loopnet.com/company/longpoint-property-group/east-rutherford-nj/v0r2qdfl/; https://cisleads.com/company/details/412123). In NJ since at least 2020, it buys infill multi-tenant small-bay parks, flex and cold-storage buildings and owner-occupier sale-leasebacks of 30,000-507,000 SF in Morris, Passaic, Essex, Union and Middlesex counties, leases up and sells in 3-4 years (Clifton: bought 2022-09, sold 2025-11). NY/NJ is a named Fund III primary market (https://longpoint.com/news-insights/longpoint-closes-on-second-largest-us-industrial-fund-this-year/).

## 2. Stated buy box
- Stated: "acquire underperforming industrial assets in supply-constrained infill locations" (pack A row, https://longpoint.com/about-us/?location=newjersey); it buys "infill industrial warehouses at attractive prices to execute value-add strategies" (https://longpoint.com/strategy/).
- Stated structures: physical repositioning, adaptive reuse, development; grocery-anchored retail runs in a separate fund (pack).
- No published size, price, clear-height, age or tenancy criteria - pattern inferred from deals (INFERRED): 30,000-200,000 SF single buildings or 100,000-510,000 SF parks; multi-tenant small-bay with short WALT (Montville: 52 tenants, WALT under 2.9 years) or single-tenant sale-leasebacks; older product on I-80 / Route 46 / Route 3 / I-78; ~$184-191/SF basis.

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Priced NJ deals run $18.5M (98,000 SF) to $81.8M (445,000 SF) at $184-191/SF; Piscataway ($19.4M, 2025) sits in the same band and Totowa's ~$64M loan implies a check above $65M. Three 30,000-40,000 SF buildings show they also do sub-$10M fill-ins (prices not found). Mix: 3 multi-building parks from institutional sellers, 2 sale-leasebacks, 3 small single-asset buys; acquisition debt used (Bank United, PGIM); off-market accepted (Piscataway). Repeat brokers: C&W, Avison Young, Lee & Associates NJ, IPRG.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Size: 30,000-200,000 SF single building or 100,000-500,000+ SF park; roughly $5M-$85M.
- Location: North/Central NJ infill - Morris, Passaic, Essex, Union, Middlesex - on I-80, Route 46, Route 3, I-78, Route 287; not exurban greenfield.
- Tenancy: multi-tenant small-bay with short WALT and below-market rents, or an owner-occupier open to a sale-leaseback; vacancy from a departing owner-occupier is acceptable (Hillside).
- Physical: older warehouse/flex/light-manufacturing with dock loading; refrigerated space fine; excess land a plus (Totowa).
- Price: ~$185-190/SF basis; value-add returns, not core cap rates.
- Timing: Fund IV first close targeted 2026-04 means fresh equity in 2026; off-market and institutional-seller deals both work.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Has Fund IV had its first close, how much is earmarked for NY/NJ industrial versus multifamily and manufactured housing, and what hold period does it underwrite given the 3-year Clifton round-trip?

3. How much day-one vacancy will you take, and do IOS yards, cold storage (as at Fairfield) or manufacturer sale-leasebacks fit Fund IV?
4. Which NJ submarkets are the 2026 targets - still Morris/Passaic/Essex, or Middlesex/Exit 8A and Bergen/Hudson too?
