# Marcus Partners - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Marcus Partners is a Boston-based, vertically integrated private-equity real estate firm investing through closed-end value-add funds; its manager, Marcus Partners, LLC, is an SEC-registered adviser (CRD 161326) at One Boston Place, 201 Washington St, Suite 2100, Boston (pack: https://adviserinfo.sec.gov/firm/summary/161326). Capital is institutional (endowments, foundations, pensions, family offices): Fund IV closed at about $650M in 2022 (pack: https://therealdeal.com/new-york/tristate/2022/09/22/pe-firm-marcus-partners-buys-south-plainfield-warehouses-for-37m/) and Marcus Capital Partners Fund V closed 2026-03 at $875M, above its $750M target and $850M hard cap, with a primary focus on industrial and multifamily (https://marcuspartners.com/mp-blog/marcus-partners-completes-fundraising-for-largest-fund-to-date-at-875-million). Stated footprint is Boston, New York and Washington, D.C. metros, though Fund V's first two deals were in Atlanta, so the map is widening (https://www.commercialsearch.com/news/marcus-partners-closes-largest-fund-at-875m/). No NJ office found in public sources. Active in NJ since 2021: it buys 90,000-200,000 SF Class B warehouses and low-coverage / outdoor-storage sites along I-287 and I-80 (Paterson, South Plainfield, Piscataway, Boonton), recapped a 1.2M SF South Jersey small-bay portfolio in 2024, and in 2025 moved into ground-up with an office-to-warehouse teardown in Whippany. Hold is value-add, not long-term: the Paterson pair bought in 2021 was sold in 2024-05 (https://re-nj.com/cw-marcus-partners-sells-192000-sq-ft-paterson-industrial-portfolio/).

## 2. Stated buy box
- Product / geography (stated 2022-09): "portfolio primarily consists of industrial, life science and multifamily assets in the Boston, New York and Washington, D.C. metro areas" (pack: https://therealdeal.com/new-york/tristate/2022/09/22/pe-firm-marcus-partners-buys-south-plainfield-warehouses-for-37m/).
- Fund V mandate (stated 2026-03): value-add, "primary focus on industrial and multifamily properties, while retaining flexibility to pursue opportunities across other asset classes" (https://marcuspartners.com/mp-blog/marcus-partners-completes-fundraising-for-largest-fund-to-date-at-875-million).
- Metro NY mandate (stated 2026): sourcing "acquisition and development opportunities in the industrial and multifamily sectors across the Tri-State Metro area as well as Metro Philadelphia" (https://marcuspartners.com/mp-blog/marcus-partners-adds-alex-schaefer-to-lead-acquisitions-for-metro-new-york).
- Size, price, clear height, age, tenancy: no published criteria - pattern inferred from deals: 90,000-200,000 SF 1970s-80s warehouses (multi- or single-tenant) with excess land / IOS (1-17 acres) in Middlesex, Morris and Passaic; accepts near-term vacancy (Boonton) and short WALT; one public price, $36.5M (INFERRED).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Only one NJ price is public: $36.5M for 200,000 SF ($182/SF) at Harmich Rd (pack). Single-asset buys ran 89,070-200,000 SF; the Camber deal was a partial-interest recap of a 1.2M SF portfolio, terms undisclosed (https://re-nj.com/marcus-partners-acquires-stake-in-1-2-million-sq-ft-south-jersey-pennsylvania-light-industrial-portfolio-in-recap-deal-with-camber/). Shape: single assets bought direct, off-market where possible (Harmich), with the two most recent done as JVs with local operators (Ridgecut Road, Camber) and third-party debt (Blackstone, Truist). INFERRED check: $15-50M per asset; Fund V's $875M allows larger portfolio bids.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- 75,000-250,000 SF existing warehouse, or 3-20 acres of low-coverage / IOS, in Middlesex, Morris, Passaic, Somerset or Burlington along I-287, I-80 or the NJ Turnpike.
- Value-add story: 1970s-80s building, below-market rents, near-term vacancy or short WALT, excess land for trailers, outdoor storage or expansion.
- Obsolete office or flex sites that can be razed for a 36' clear warehouse (Whippany model).
- Price $15-50M single asset (inferred); portfolio recaps with a local partner at larger scale.
- Off-market or lightly marketed; open to JV with an owner-operator who brings the deal.
- Timing: Fund V closed 2026-03 with only two deals done - fresh capital to deploy.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. How much of Fund V's $875M is still uncommitted for the Northeast, and what hold period are you underwriting - a 3-year flip like Paterson or longer?
2. Which NJ submarkets and size bands are you targeting for 2026 - I-287 Middlesex, Morris / I-80, South Jersey small-bay - and is there any appetite for cold storage or IOS-only land without a building?
3. How much vacancy will you buy - fully vacant like Boonton - and what in-place yield or rent gap do you need to make a value-add deal work?
4. Who signs off on an NJ deal, how long from LOI to close, and can you go hard non-refundable quickly on an off-market deal as at Harmich Rd?
5. Who should NJ deals go to - Alex Schaefer's Metro New York team, Todd Minerley, or JV partners like Ridgecut Road and Camber - and which brokers already show you product?
