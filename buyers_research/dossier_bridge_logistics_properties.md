# Bridge Logistics Properties - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Bridge Logistics Properties (BLP) is the closed-end private-equity logistics arm of Bridge Investment Group, launched 2021-06 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210617005751/en/Bridge-Investment-Group-Launches-Bridge-Logistics-Properties); Bridge became an Apollo affiliate when Apollo closed its ~$1.5B purchase in 2025-09, keeping the brand and teams (https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/apollo-completes-acquisition-bridge-investment-group-2025-09-02). Parent HQ is Salt Lake City, the platform lists Newport Beach, CA, and the Northeast runs from an East Rutherford, NJ office (street address not found), one of four regional offices (https://www.bridgeblp.com/). In NJ it buys existing infill warehouses with yards or excess land for value-add / core-plus repositioning - Linden (2022), Piscataway (2022), Hasbrouck Heights (2023) - and re-nj says the Northeast office held six NJ properties plus a development site by 2022, so the file below is partial (https://re-nj.com/industrial-buyer-adds-two-building-170000-sq-ft-linden-warehouse-portfolio/). It is not Bridge Industrial, the Perth Amboy / Newark developer. It leases up and holds: at Hasbrouck Heights it renewed Skyline Windows and Direct Freight Express (87,000+ SF) (https://njbiz.com/north-central-jersey-industrial-sites-draw-leasing-activity/) and leased 24,267 SF to NAVAC, reaching 100% occupancy (https://re-nj.com/bridge-logistics-hvac-manufacturer-sign-24000-sq-ft-industrial-lease-in-teterboro/).

## 2. Stated buy box
- Strategy (2022-07): "value-add and Core-Plus acquisitions of logistics assets as well as ground-up development opportunities predominantly in infill, last-mile driven markets" (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220718005322/en).
- Fund II (2026-06): "acquiring and repositioning high-quality logistics real estate assets in supply-constrained U.S. infill markets and global gateway cities," disciplined basis, off-market or selectively marketed (https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/02/3305396/0/en/bridge-logistics-properties-raises-nearly-1-4-billion-for-its-value-fund-ii-exceeding-1-billion-target.html).
- Size, price, clear height, age, tenancy: no published criteria - pattern inferred from deals: 60,000-200,000 SF one- or two-building warehouses, Class A 36-ft clear down to Class B dock/grade, always with yard or excess land (0.8-14.65 ac), Union / Middlesex / Bergen counties, off-market, $45M-$90M (INFERRED).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Priced NJ deals ran $45.0M-$89.5M for 170,000-202,000 SF ($223-$526/SF); Piscataway (59,572 SF, undisclosed) widens the SF band to roughly 60,000-200,000 SF. Single-asset or two buildings on one street, bought outright by the fund with life-company debt, no JVs or sale-leasebacks seen. Two of three were off-market, and the platform closed 21 deals / $789M in its first seven months, so expect fast execution; Fund II's ~$1.4B close (2026-06) means fresh equity.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- 50,000-250,000 SF existing warehouse, one or two buildings.
- Infill Northern / Central NJ: Meadowlands, Port Newark-Elizabeth-Linden, Turnpike 8A-12.
- Dock-high, 24-ft+ clear, yard or excess land of 0.5+ acres; cold storage acceptable.
- Vacant or short-WALT multi-tenant (value-add) or stabilized (core-plus) both fit.
- $40M-$100M, $200-$550/SF basis seen in 2022-2023.
- Off-market or lightly marketed, direct principal process.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. How much of Fund II's ~$1.4B is uncommitted, what is the hold period, and is there a Northeast allocation?
2. Since the 2025-09 Apollo closing, what changed in approvals, return hurdles or access to Apollo co-invest for larger NJ deals?
3. Which NJ submarkets and size bands are 2026 priorities, and is there appetite for vacancy, IOS / trailer yards or cold storage?
4. Who in East Rutherford screens deals, who signs off, and what is the typical LOI-to-close timeline?
5. How do you prefer to see deals - direct, through specific brokers - and are any 2022-2023 NJ assets for sale?
