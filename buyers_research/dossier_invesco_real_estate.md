# Invesco Real Estate - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Invesco Real Estate is the real-estate arm of Invesco Ltd., investing through open-end funds; its core vehicle Invesco Core Real Estate - U.S.A., L.P. (ICRE, formed 2004, ~$9.5B) and manager Invesco Realty, Inc. are at 2001 Ross Avenue, Suite 3400, Dallas, TX (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1305319/000130531920000001/primary_doc.xml). No NJ office found in public sources. Active in NJ industrial since 2023, it buys stabilized modern big-box directly (Exit 8A, Livingston) and does value-add and ground-up deals in JVs with Hampshire Cos. (Meadowlands) and Camber Real Estate Partners (Burlington County) (pack; https://www.roi-nj.com/2025/01/27/real_estate_development/calling-it-a-region-on-the-rise-camber-invesco-buy-industrial-portfolio-in-burlington-county/). It took a minority stake in Hoboken-based Faropoint in 2023-11 (https://roi-nj.com/2023/11/29/real_estate/faropoint-secures-strategic-investment-from-invesco-real-estate). Core-plus holder that will trade: the Teterboro JV asset was resold about two years after purchase (https://re-nj.com/reports-hampshire-invesco-sell-teterboro-warehouse-for-75-million-in-deal-with-principal/).

## 2. Stated buy box
- No published criteria - pattern inferred from deals:
- INFERRED size: 190,000-385,000 SF per deal; single buildings 216K-294K SF, one 5-building portfolio (pack deal rows; roi-nj 2025 above).
- INFERRED product: cross-dock / rear-load warehouse, 30-36 ft clear, 1991 or newer; multi-tenant light industrial; development with a partner (pack; https://njbiz.com/34-5m-financing-secured-for-bergen-industrial-development/).
- INFERRED submarkets: Exit 8A, Essex County, Meadowlands, Burlington County / Exit 5 (pack A row).
- Tenancy: either - single-tenant (South Brunswick, Teterboro) and multi-tenant (Lumberton/Hainesport).
- 2026 house view: "industrial remains well positioned given tight vacancy and resilient tenant demand" (https://www.invesco.com/us/en/insights/renewed-potential-for-real-estate-investors-in-2026.html).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Priced NJ buys run $33.5M-$98.5M ($151-$335/SF) on 187,530-384,335 SF; direct stabilized buys were $55M and $98.5M, JV deals smaller. Mostly single-asset, one 5-building portfolio. Direct for core product; JV equity with Hampshire and Camber for value-add and development, with third-party debt. All priced deals were broker-marketed (JLL, C&W, CBRE); no off-market, 1031 or sale-leaseback signals found.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- 180,000-400,000 SF single building, or a 3-5 building light-industrial park of similar total.
- Exit 8A, Essex County, Meadowlands or Burlington County / Exit 5, near Turnpike interchanges.
- 30 ft+ clear, cross-dock or deep rear-load, 1990s or newer; new construction welcome.
- Credit 3PL/logistics tenant, or multi-tenant with upside if Hampshire, Camber or Faropoint is in the deal.
- $30M-$100M; $150-$335/SF.
- Marketed process; expect fund-committee timing, not a 30-day close.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Which vehicle (ICRE, U.S. Income Fund, separate account) is buying NJ industrial in 2026, how much undeployed equity does it hold, and what is its target hold period?
2. Will you underwrite vacancy, outdoor storage or cold storage, or only stabilized dry warehouse?
3. Which NJ submarkets are on the 2026 list - still Exit 8A, Meadowlands and Exit 5, or expanding?
4. Who signs off on an NJ deal and how long from LOI to committee approval to closing?
5. Do you want deals direct, or routed through Hampshire, Camber or Faropoint - and who at Invesco covers the Northeast?
