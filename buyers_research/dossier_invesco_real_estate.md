# Invesco Real Estate - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Invesco Real Estate is the real-estate arm of Invesco Ltd., investing mainly through open-end commingled funds; its core vehicle, Invesco Core Real Estate - U.S.A., L.P. (ICRE, formed 2004, ~$9.5B), and its manager Invesco Realty, Inc. are based at 2001 Ross Avenue, Suite 3400, Dallas, TX (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1305319/000130531920000001/primary_doc.xml). No NJ office was found in public sources. In NJ it has been visible since 2022-2023 and buys two ways: directly for stabilized, modern big-box product (Exit 8A cross-dock, new-build Livingston logistics center) and through JVs with NJ operating partners (The Hampshire Cos. in the Meadowlands, Camber Real Estate Partners in Burlington County) for value-add and ground-up deals (pack; https://www.roi-nj.com/2025/01/27/real_estate_development/calling-it-a-region-on-the-rise-camber-invesco-buy-industrial-portfolio-in-burlington-county/). It also took a minority stake in Hoboken-based last-mile operator Faropoint in 2023-11 via its Platform Investments team (https://roi-nj.com/2023/11/29/real_estate/faropoint-secures-strategic-investment-from-invesco-real-estate). Hold strategy is core-plus, but it will trade: the Teterboro JV asset was resold roughly two years after purchase (https://re-nj.com/reports-hampshire-invesco-sell-teterboro-warehouse-for-75-million-in-deal-with-principal/).

## 2. Stated buy box
- No published criteria - pattern inferred from deals:
- INFERRED size: 190,000-385,000 SF per deal (single buildings 216K-294K SF; one 5-building portfolio) (pack deal rows; roi-nj 2025 above).
- INFERRED product: cross-dock / rear-load warehouse, 30-36 ft clear, built 1991 or newer, plus multi-tenant light industrial in business-park settings and ground-up development with a partner (pack; https://njbiz.com/34-5m-financing-secured-for-bergen-industrial-development/).
- INFERRED submarkets: Exit 8A (South Brunswick), Essex County (Livingston), Meadowlands (Teterboro, Bogota, Ridgefield Park), Burlington County / Exit 5 (pack A row).
- Tenancy: either - 100% single-tenant leased (South Brunswick, Teterboro) and multi-tenant (Lumberton/Hainesport) (pack; roi-nj 2025).
- House view 2026: "industrial remains well positioned given tight vacancy and resilient tenant demand" (https://www.invesco.com/us/en/insights/renewed-potential-for-real-estate-investors-in-2026.html).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Priced NJ buys range $33.5M-$98.5M ($151-$335/SF) on 187,530-384,335 SF; the two direct, stabilized buys were $55M and $98.5M, the JV deals smaller. Mostly single-asset; one 5-building portfolio. Direct all-cash-style acquisitions for core product; JV equity with Hampshire and Camber for value-add and development, with third-party acquisition or construction debt. All four priced deals were broker-marketed (JLL, C&W, CBRE) - no off-market, 1031 or sale-leaseback signals found.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- 180,000-400,000 SF single building, or a 3-5 building light-industrial park of similar total size.
- Exit 8A, Essex County, Meadowlands or Burlington County / Exit 5; near Turnpike interchanges.
- 30 ft+ clear, cross-dock or deep rear-load, 1990s or newer; new construction welcome.
- Leased to a credit 3PL/logistics user, or multi-tenant with value-add upside if a partner (Hampshire, Camber, Faropoint) is in the deal.
- Price $30M-$100M; $150-$335/SF.
- Marketed process with institutional brokers; expect fund-level committee timing, not a 30-day close.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Which vehicle (ICRE, U.S. Income Fund, a separate account) is buying NJ industrial in 2026, how much undeployed equity does it have, and what is its target hold?
2. Will you underwrite vacancy, outdoor storage or cold storage, or only stabilized dry warehouse?
3. Which NJ submarkets are on the 2026 target list - still Exit 8A, Meadowlands and Exit 5, or expanding?
4. Who signs off on an NJ deal and how long from LOI to committee approval to closing?
5. Do you prefer deals sourced direct, or routed through Hampshire, Camber or Faropoint - and who at Invesco covers the Northeast?
