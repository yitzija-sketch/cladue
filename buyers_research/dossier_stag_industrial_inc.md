# STAG Industrial, Inc. - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Boston-based, publicly traded industrial REIT that buys and holds single- and multi-tenant warehouse and light-manufacturing buildings nationwide; no NJ office found in public sources (A row; https://www.stagindustrial.com/acquisitions/). It has owned NJ product since before its 2011 IPO, when the legacy "GI" portfolio brought in Piscataway and Lopatcong (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1479094/000104746911003745/a2203462z424b4.htm). The FY2023 10-K lists 13 NJ buildings across Somerset, Middlesex, Burlington, Gloucester and Salem counties - a Central Jersey (I-287) and South Jersey (I-295 / Turnpike Exits 2-6) footprint, not Port or Meadowlands (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1479094/000147909424000007/stag-20231231.htm). Press-visible NJ buys are 100k-120k SF single-tenant warehouse/flex plus a 563,000 SF Class A Westampton warehouse (2022-09, A row notes). Long-term holder that prunes selectively: 2026 guidance is $350M-$650M of acquisitions against $100M-$200M of dispositions (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stag-industrial-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results-302685587.html).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
NJ deals cluster at 101k-120k SF; the one disclosed price is $26.5M ($261/SF). Company-wide checks run larger: 2025 averaged $34.5M and ~292k SF per building; Q4-2025 was 7 buildings for $285.9M at a 6.47% cash cap (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stag-industrial-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results-302685587.html); Q1-2026 was one 748,833 SF building for $80.7M (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stag-industrial-announces-first-quarter-2026-results-302756019.html). Single assets dominate; all-cash balance-sheet buyer, takes sale-leasebacks, buys broker-marketed deals; 2026 guidance 6.25%-6.75% cash caps, back-end weighted.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Size: single building ~100k-600k SF; NJ history 100k-120k.
- Submarket: Central Jersey I-287 / Exit 8A-10 or South Jersey I-295 / Turnpike Exits 2-6.
- Physical: Class A or B warehouse/distribution or light manufacturing; 24'+ clear, dock-high, ESFR; flex accepted at Branchburg.
- Tenancy: single-tenant net lease or sale-leaseback; partial or full vacancy OK.
- Price: $5M-$100M near a 6.25%-6.75% cash cap.
- Timing: more volume expected 2H-2026; listed deals fine.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Of the $350M-$650M 2026 guidance, how much is uncommitted for 2H-2026, and does NJ or the Northeast carry a target share?
2. How much vacancy will you underwrite in NJ - a fully vacant 120k SF Burlington County box or only partial - and at what cap-rate discount to a leased deal?
3. Any appetite for IOS, cold storage or office-heavy flex like Branchburg, or warehouse and light manufacturing only?
4. Which NJ submarkets are on the 2026 list - Central, Burlington/Gloucester, or Northern NJ and the Meadowlands - and what did you pass on recently and why?
5. Who sources NJ deals (your team, local brokers, principals), what is the IC process and LOI-to-close timeline, and are any NJ assets in the $100M-$200M disposition program?
