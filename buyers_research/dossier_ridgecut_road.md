# Ridgecut Road - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Ridgecut Road (legal name Ridgecut Road LLC) is a private IOS specialist - it buys low-coverage industrial outdoor storage and industrial service facility (ISF) sites - based in New York, NY and founded 2021/early 2022 by twin brothers Scott and Eric Shalek (pack: https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/2259521D:US; https://re-nj.com/shalek-brothers-identical-twins-making-their-mark-in-low-coverage-industrial-with-ground-up-development-on-the-horizon/). No NJ office or HQ street address was found in public sources. Capital is deal-by-deal JV equity from institutional partners plus bank acquisition loans; no SEC Form D located (pack). First NJ buy was spring 2023 (South Plainfield, with Marcus Partners); by 2026-07 it held roughly a dozen NJ yards of 1.7-10 acres with 8,000-39,000 SF service buildings, mostly in Middlesex and Union counties along I-287 / Route 1 / Turnpike, with outliers in Aberdeen, Lincoln Park and Fairfield. It owns, operates and re-leases, has recapitalised two I-287 properties (https://re-nj.com/ridgecut-road-recaps-low-coverage-industrial-properties-in-i-287-corridor/) and separately develops Class A warehouses (146,075 SF project in Montgomery, NY).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Disclosed 2023-2024 prices run $9.65M-$23.45M; the three disclosed 2026 buys stepped up to $21.7M-$37M. Sites 2.5-10 acres, buildings 10,000-38,800 SF; computed land pricing rises from ~$2.3M/acre (Sayreville 2023) to ~$4.4-4.7M/acre (2026 deals). All single-asset; about half are JVs with institutional equity, and every disclosed deal carried bank debt. It buys from owner-users (truck dealer, equipment dealer, striping contractor), accepted a 6-month sale-leaseback and a seller's 1031; JLL is the usual intermediary, often on both sides.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- 1.5-10 acre low-coverage site (under ~20% coverage), paved and fenced, zoned for outdoor storage / truck parking.
- 8,000-40,000 SF service or maintenance building with drive-in bays; 15-28 ft clear acceptable; age not a filter.
- Middlesex or Union County first, within ~2 miles of I-287, Route 1 or the Turnpike; Monmouth, Morris, Essex secondary.
- Trucking, fleet-maintenance or equipment-rental tenancy; leased and vacant-for-release both bought.
- $8M-$40M single asset; owner-user sellers, sale-leasebacks and 1031 sellers all fit; bank-financed, so assume a 60-90 day close.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. Is 2026 capital a discretionary vehicle or still deal-by-deal JV equity, and how much remains to deploy in NJ over the next 12 months?
2. Are the 2023-2024 assets being recapitalised or sold at their 3-year loan maturities, or held long term?
3. How far will they go on vacancy, unpaved land, zoning risk or cold storage versus fully leased IOS?
4. Which 2026-2027 submarkets beyond I-287 are on the list - Morris/Essex, Monmouth, Bergen, Orange County NY - and what is the minimum acreage?
5. Who screens inbound deals (Scott or Eric), how fast to LOI, and do they prefer off-market owner-user leads or marketed processes?
