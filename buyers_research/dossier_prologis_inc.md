# Prologis, Inc. - NJ industrial buyer dossier
*Prepared 2026-10-11 from buyers_research (A/B/C files) + 7 gap-filling searches. Confidence: med overall.*

## 1. Who they are and what they do in NJ
Prologis is the largest public logistics REIT (NYSE: PLD, Maryland corp., HQ San Francisco, SEC CIK 1045609), buying off its balance sheet and through its open-end co-investment fund USLF (pack: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045609/000110465922088617/tm2222886d1_8k.htm). The NJ/NY regional office is One Meadowlands Plaza, East Rutherford, NJ 07073 (pack A row). It is NJ's dominant owner: 53.4M SF owned or controlled, No. 1 on NJBIZ's 2026 developer list (https://njbiz.com/prologis-leads-njbiz-2026-commercial-real-estate-developers/), ~56M SF per RE-NJ in 2026 (https://re-nj.com/prologis-completes-new-437000-sq-ft-warehouse-in-i-287-corridor/). Much of that came via M&A - Liberty Property Trust (2020-02) and Duke Realty (2022-10, adding 8.3M SF plus ~3M SF of NJ development and land) (pack: https://re-nj.com/prologis-adds-8-3-million-sq-ft-in-new-jersey-with-more-to-come-after-duke-realty-deal/). Property-level buys cluster in Meadowlands/Teterboro (Bergen) and Exit 8A / I-287 (Middlesex), plus land and conversion sites (Perth Amboy, Mount Laurel, Gloucester Co.); a long-term core holder that develops, leases and keeps.

## 2. Stated buy box
- No published criteria - pattern inferred from deals: Class A big-box and infill last-mile logistics of 100K-600K SF, multi-building portfolios, IOS / covered land and office-to-logistics conversion sites, in Meadowlands, Exit 8A, the Turnpike/port corridor and now South Jersey (INFERRED from pack deal rows; pack A-row confidence "med").
- Corporate stance 2026: Q2 2026 release reports $1.8B of third-party acquisitions "at attractive discounts to replacement cost", $1.6B of logistics and data-center starts, and acquisition guidance (Prologis share) raised to a reported $1.5-2.0B from $1.0-1.5B - verify column order in the release (https://www.prologis.com/insights-news/press-releases/prologis-reports-second-quarter-2026-results).
- Regional quote: Mortimer calls NJ "one of the East Coast's most important logistics hubs" (https://re-nj.com/prologis-eyes-early-2027-delivery-for-new-379000-sq-ft-bergen-county-warehouse/).

## 4. Typical check size and deal shape
Priced single-asset buys run $5.55M to $166.8M on 100K-600K SF buildings; the $12.2M-$39.6M band is Liberty-era infill, post-2022 checks skew to $150M+ big-box and land (computed from Section 3). Portfolios range from a two-building pair ($197.4M) to entity M&A (Duke, 8.3M SF in NJ). All-cash balance-sheet buyer (pack, INFERRED) that also does IOS sale-leasebacks (FleetSource) and buys land and office sites to develop. Deals were broker-marketed (CBRE, NAI Hanson, Avison Young); brokers often did not name the buyer.

## 7. What would make a property match them
- Size: 100K-600K SF buildings, or 10-200 ac of land / covered land / IOS with development upside.
- Submarket: Meadowlands-Teterboro-Carlstadt; Exit 8A-Cranbury-Monroe; I-287 Piscataway-Edison-Somerset; Perth Amboy / port corridor; Gloucester Co.
- Tenancy: leased single- or multi-tenant (600 Ridge Rd, 200-250 Central were 100% leased); IOS sale-leaseback accepted.
- Specs: modern cross-dock, 32'-40' clear; older infill works if the dirt is irreplaceable (Lodi, Teterboro).
- Price: $5M infill to $200M portfolios; post-2022 sweet spot is $100M+ or developable land.
- Timing: balance-sheet cash, no financing contingency; broker-marketed processes welcome.

## 8. Five questions for the first call
1. For 2026-2027, is NJ acquisition capital coming from the balance sheet or USLF, and does the raised $1.5-2.0B acquisition guidance change your NJ allocation?
2. Will you underwrite vacant or short-WALT buildings in the Meadowlands and Exit 8A, or only stabilized assets and development?
3. Is there appetite for IOS / truck terminals and cold storage, or was the FleetSource sale-leaseback a one-off?
4. Beyond Bergen and Gloucester, which NJ submarkets are you targeting for land and conversions in 2026, and at what $/buildable SF?
5. Who screens inbound deals - Mortimer's East Rutherford team or Denver/SF - and what is the LOI-to-close timeline for a $50M-$150M single asset?
